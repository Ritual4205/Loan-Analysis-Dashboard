Your Rights Under the Law

⚖️ Legal Protection

Helpers are not liable for injury or death of victims while providing assistance in good faith.

🚫 No Harassment

Police or hospitals cannot force you to reveal your identity or stay against your will.

💙 Free & Voluntary

You can remain anonymous and are not bound to pay hospital or medical costs.

Overview

The Samaritan Law protects and encourages individuals who assist in emergencies. It ensures people acting in good faith are not penalized for unintentional harm caused while helping others.

Key Point: Acting as a Good Samaritan is both a moral duty and a legal protection.

Basic Rules

  • Offer immediate help to anyone in distress or emergency situations.
  • Do not expect compensation unless provided by law.
  • Act with good faith, care, and caution.
  • Stay with the person until help arrives.
  • Call emergency services as soon as possible.

Government Guidelines

  • Good Samaritans are legally protected when assisting accident victims.
  • Doctors and responders must follow proper medical aid procedures.
  • Maintain a brief record of your help, if possible.
  • Encourage awareness in schools, workplaces, and public areas.

For more information, visit the official government website or local authorities.
